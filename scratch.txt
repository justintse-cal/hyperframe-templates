Chart Card
use it when you have a screenshot of a chart to highlight
- the chart screenshot slide in from the right side from a 3D perspective (2s)
- Then a highlight outlined box fade in to highlight the important element in the chart with additional text box fade in too to display highlighted message (2s)
- At the same time, the rest of the content/area is dimmed using a black color overlay with 50% opacity
- no exit animation
- use the ai_price.jpg file in stock media folder as the placeholder
